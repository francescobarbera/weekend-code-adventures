# The €144 Bureaucracy Adventure

I recently recovered €144 from a subscription I had activated by mistake.

The interesting part? An AI agent helped me do it.

Not by performing some extraordinary technical trick. It helped me navigate the small bureaucratic maze between me and my money.

Emails, policies, procedures, carefully worded requests. The sort of adventure nobody wants to have.

I reviewed the steps, approved the actions, and let AI handle much of the tedious work.

We often imagine AI agents writing software, running companies, or making scientific discoveries.

But perhaps one of their greatest achievements will be something much less glamorous: making bureaucracy bearable.

Bureaucracy is an interface designed for organizations, not people. AI might finally become our interpreter.

Sometimes progress looks like getting your €144 back.
